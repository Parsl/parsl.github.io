APeX Summit 2026 — Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute (Hybrid) — September 14–15, 2026

The APeX Summit (Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute) is the eighth annual community meeting, bringing together researchers, developers, and cyberinfrastructure experts worldwide to discuss experiences using and developing Academy, Parsl, and Globus Compute. The summit will be held as a hybrid meeting on September 14–15, 2026, with the in-person component in Chicago.

Registration

Join us for our annual APeX Summit (Academy, Parsl, Globus Compute), September 14–15, 2026. The hybrid summit brings together researchers, users, developers, and administrators using Academy, Parsl, and Globus Compute for two days of lightning talks and discussion. Join us online or in Chicago: register here.

We include a nominal fee for in-person registration to cover expenses, please email Kyle Chard if you need financial assistance
